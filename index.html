shorts-uploader

A personal, single-user tool that uploads the short educational videos I make to my own YouTube channel and Facebook Page, and schedules when each one is released.

Uses YouTube API Services Uses the Meta Graph API

What it does

  • Every video is researched, scripted and watched in full by me before it can be uploaded. The tool refuses to upload anything I have not approved.
  • It uploads approved videos with their title, description, tags and thumbnail.
  • It schedules releases at fixed times, at most two a day.
  • It checks my own recent uploads so the same video is never uploaded twice.

Who it is for

Only me. It runs on my own computer from the command line, signs in to my own accounts, and has no other users, no sign-up and no public interface.

Data

It collects no personal data from anyone. The only data it handles is about my own channel and Page: video IDs, titles and privacy status, plus the sign-in tokens Google and Meta issue to me. All of it is stored locally on my machine. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
